About Freetown City Council


Who are we


Freetown City Council (FCC) is the local government authority responsible for the management and development of Freetown, the capital city of Sierra Leone. Led by her Worship the Mayor, Yvonne Aki-Sawyerr OBE, we are committed to transforming Freetown into a vibrant, inclusive, and resilient city



Our Mission


To provide efficient, effective, and people-centerd services that improve the quality of life for all residents of Freetown.



Transform Freetown Agenda


Resilience- Tracking flooding, sanitation & climate change

Human Development- Education, healtjh and youth employment

Healthy City- Clean and green Freetown
